Oh, hi there!

Didn’t see you, but I’m glad you’re here.

My name is Ashley Calza, and I’m an actor based in Los Angeles, California.

There are only so many Just Dance routines one can perfect before my mother lovingly encourages that I expand my audience beyond the living room. Luckily for me, that nudge led exactly where I was meant to be. Acting has been a constant in my life, and somehow, I keep falling in love with it all over again.

I’m endlessly excited about where this path is taking me and deeply grateful to be doing what I love.

When I’m not performing, I’m usually collaging old magazines, working on a puzzle, or somewhere on a hiking trail pretending I'm not slightly lost.
